Macro Plate Knowledge Hub

Site Name

Macro Plate Knowledge Hub is an educational resource connected to Macro Plate Meal Prep, focused on nutrition, healthy eating, meal preparation, and wellness.

Potential website location: macroplatemealprep.com/knowledge-hub

Site Purpose

The hub will help visitors make informed decisions about nutrition and healthy eating. It will offer articles, a protein estimator, a food cost calculator, a ZIP code checker, a newsletter signup, and a way to schedule a free nutritional assessment.

Scenarios

  • How can I find nutrition articles that match my goals?
  • How much protein should I consume based on my weight and activity level?
  • Where can I find reliable information about healthy eating and meal preparation?
  • How much does my family spend on food, and could meal prep save us money?
  • Does Macro Plate Meal Prep deliver to my ZIP code?
  • Can I save articles to revisit later?
  • How can I subscribe to nutrition and wellness updates?
  • How can I schedule a free nutritional assessment?
